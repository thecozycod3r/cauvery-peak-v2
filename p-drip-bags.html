About this product

A specially curated collection of drip bags by the Masters at Cauvery Peak, featuring 3 distinctive premium single-estate coffees —Shevaroys Estate Reserve, Cauvery Peak Estate Heritage, and Glenfell Estate Classic. Experience the crisp acidity and bold character of Shevaroys Estate Reserve, the balanced richness of Cauvery Peak Estate Heritage, and the smooth, low-acidity profile of Glenfell Estate Classic.
