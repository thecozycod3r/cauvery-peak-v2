A 150-year-old coffee estate in Yercaud

ஏற்காட்டில் 150 ஆண்டு பழைமையான காபி எஸ்டேட்

A short film for BBC News Tamil, following the estate tour through to the café.

What the film shows

  1. 01One of India’s oldest coffee estates, 15 km from Yercaud, dating from British times and now in its fifth generation
  2. 02A guided tour open to the public, at ₹600 per person
  3. 03The lake built on the estate to hold the hill’s water
  4. 04A short way on, the Kombai valley
  5. 05Picking, drying and roasting, explained where each happens
  6. 06The café at the end, with the estate’s coffee in many styles

“If you’re a coffee lover, don’t miss this estate.”

You have seen the film

Now take the tour.

Ninety minutes, ₹600 a person, in your own vehicle with a coffee guide: the wet mill, the dry mill, roasting and grinding, the estate museum, and the café to finish. Closed Tuesdays.
