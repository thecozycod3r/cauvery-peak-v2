About this product

Glenfell is planted with both Arabica and Robusta varieties and inter-planted with cloves, nutmeg and pepper vines. Heavy shade provided by a mixed canopy of trees provide unique microclimatic conditions.

Brew & store

A good start is 15 g of coffee to 250 ml of water just off the boil; adjust to taste. Recipes for every brewer. Close the zip after each use and keep the pack somewhere cool and dark; whole beans stay fresh longest.
