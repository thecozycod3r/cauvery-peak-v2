Build your career with Cauvery Peak.

A working plantation, a roastery and three cafés, run by one family for five generations. Most of the work here happens outdoors, and all of it ends in a cup.

The work

Every stage, on one estate.

Cauvery Peak does not buy coffee in. Nursery, field, mills, roasting, packing, despatch, the cafés and the tours all run from the same boundary in the Shevaroy Hills — so the people who work here see the whole chain, not one link of it.

On the estate

Field and nursery

Selecting mother plants and raising seedlings, tending three years of growth before a first crop, and picking ripe cherry by hand over several rounds each season.

In the mills

Processing

Pulping on the day of picking, overnight fermentation and washing, sun-drying on the terraces, then hulling, polishing and sorting out every defective bean.

In the roastery

Roasting and despatch

Roasting only once an order exists, resting the beans for 24 hours, grinding to the customer’s method, packing and shipping straight from the estate.

With visitors

Cafés and tours

Brewing and serving at the three cafés, running the retail counters, and guiding visitors through the plantation on the Coffee Experience Tour.
