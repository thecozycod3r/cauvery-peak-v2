The Coffee Experience Tour

Follow a coffee bean from plant to cup.

A 90-minute guided journey through a 150-year-old working plantation — scenic peaks, valleys and lakes, and the bean-to-cup story told where it happens. Perfect for families, nature lovers and coffee enthusiasts alike.

What you'll see

Five stops, one bean.

  1. Drive the estate

    In your own vehicle, on all-weather roads, through scenic peaks, valleys and lakes, with a coffee guide telling the estate’s history.

  2. The wet mill

    Where ripe cherry is sorted and pulped the day it is picked, then fermented and washed.

  3. The dry mill

    Where the last two layers of skin come off and defective, damaged and broken beans are sorted out.

  4. Roasting & grinding

    Where beans are roasted only after an order has been placed.

  5. The estate museum

    The history of 150 years of coffee on this hill.

  6. The café

    Where the journey ends with the aroma of fresh coffee and a tasting of the estate’s coffees.
