Three places

Coffee is better at the source.

One café on the plantation itself, one on the main road into Yercaud, and a kiosk on the ghat road halfway up the hill.

The plantation one

Cauvery Peak Estate Café

A café on a 150-year-old coffee estate, in a quaint building with modern equipment, facing the coffee nursery. The drive up from Yercaud town is 15 km on a well-paved scenic road.

  • Freshly brewed estate coffee
  • Retail: coffee, pepper, cloves, nutmeg, mace, cardamom, honey
  • Well-appointed rest rooms
  • Parking on site
  • Starting point for the estate tour
