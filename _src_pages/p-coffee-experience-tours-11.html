About this tour

Experience coffee at its source at the Cauvery Peak Coffee Estate Tour. On this 90-minute guided journey through a 150-year-old plantation, you’ll explore scenic peaks, valleys and lakes while tracing the bean-to-cup story. Travel in your own vehicle on all-weather roads as a coffee guide shares the estate’s history and craft.

Good to know

  • The tour starts and ends at the Cauvery Peak café, 15 km (about a 25-minute drive) from Yercaud Lake.
  • The route is about 2.5 km on all-weather roads, fine for any car or bike.
  • Every stop is easy to reach for children and older visitors.
  • The mills and processing units run with the season, so what is working on the day varies.
