Before you order

When will it arrive?

We roast after you order and dispatch within 3–5 business days. Standard delivery then takes 5–7 business days within India, with tracking by email or WhatsApp. Shipping is free.

Which grind should I choose?

Whole beans stay fresh longest if you have a grinder. Otherwise pick how you brew and we grind it for that method. The grind guide shows each one.

How do I keep it fresh?

Close the zip after each use and keep the pack somewhere cool and dark. Whole beans hold their flavour longer than ground coffee, so grind just before brewing if you can.

What if it arrives damaged?

Tell us within 48 hours with a photo and we replace it or refund in full. Returns policy.

Is it really one estate?

Each single estate comes from one of the family’s three estates on the same hill. The blends are drawn from all three. Everything is grown, processed, roasted and packed on the estate.
