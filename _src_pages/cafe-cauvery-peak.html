Cauvery Peak Estate Café

A café in a quaint estate building with modern equipment, on the roadside with parking, looking out over the coffee plant nursery. The drive from Yercaud town is 15 km on a well-paved, scenic road.

Order the estate’s own coffees and specialty drinks, then shop the retail section for the coffee, pepper, cloves, nutmeg, mace, cardamom and honey grown on the same land.

This is also where the Coffee Experience Tour begins: a guide walks you through cultivation, harvesting, processing and roasting, and you finish with a tasting.

  • Freshly brewed estate coffee and specialty drinks
  • Retail: coffee, spices and honey from the estate
  • Well-appointed rest rooms
  • Parking on site
  • Starting point for the estate tour
